# src/game notes

- Champions holding a weapon (e.g. Miriam) use `weapon` layers in `allies.ts` (body / whole weapon / grip-hand overlay): the walk only steps the body's feet band and the weapon rotates intact around its grip, so it is never sliced. Test Map champion toggles are generated from `ALLY_POOL` and recruit through `summonCompanion`.
- The Agile Soldier special dash is a committed state in `enemyTick` that returns early (direction locked at start, fixed travel distance past Moses); hitting Moses only applies damage/FX. Any branch that falls through to the hop logic while dashing re-aims at Moses and makes him look stuck.
- Moses has two visual versions switched by `MOSES_VISUAL_VERSION` in `game/mosesGameArt.ts` ("original" keeps the old sprite/staff/animation; "new" uses the `moses-v2-*` layers). Gameplay reads only `MOSES_ART`/`MOSES_ATTACK`, so switching never affects combat.
- Champions retreat between attacks via `allyRetreatDir` (inverse-distance threat vector, obstacle-probed headings) recomputed with the target every `ALLY_RETARGET_SECONDS`.
- Moses v2 free arm is split at runtime (`freeArmLayers`); adjust `ARM` if body art changes.
- Flies are a projectile shield (`syncOrbitFlies` removes `enemyOwned` projectiles, no damage). Boils = `boilstrail` emitter dropping one-shot `boilbubble`s; Livestock targets `LIVESTOCK_TARGET_KINDS`; hazards with `bleedOnce` show blood once per enemy.
- Hail alternates ice/fire via `state.hailFirePhase` (toggled only on the main cast; multi-attack repeats reuse it); "fire" stays in `PLAGUES` but is not in `PLAGUE_ORDER`. Reworked plague visuals live in `plagueFx.ts`.
- Red Sea casts through `state.redSeaCast` (raise → strike → walls); staff attacks wait via `redSeaCasting`. Its center burst runs on a `redseatimer` hazard (game time), never `setTimeout`.
- Pillar of Cloud cages via enemy `data.cage*` clamped after all movement; Pillar of Fire burns via `burnUntil`/`burnZone` (single damage source per zone).
- Ramses art switches: `RAMSES_VISUAL_VERSION` (ramsesArt.ts: v2 walk + seated-with-throne; procedural throne hidden while he sits) and `RAMSES_CHARIOT_VERSION` (ramsesChariotArt.ts). Old assets stay for rollback; never delete them.
- Route refreshes (enemy target choice, champion target + retreat heading, wolf pack centre) go through `routing.ts` (`ROUTE_MIN_INTERVAL` 0.4 s per character); per-frame steering toward the cached target is not a refresh.
- Firstborn branches start at the Angel of Light's sword (`angelArt.ts`, `firstbornangel` visual hazard anchored at cast). Livestock is a camera-following `livestockwave` band.
- Supplied champions use virtual sprite coordinates and original-pixel arm/weapon layers; draw full-resolution sources into those coordinates without changing their proportions.
- Champion projectile simulation stays on the ground; hand height is a visual lift only, keeping collision aligned with enemy ground positions.
- Support timelines run without hostile targets; verify healing and wards in an empty arena as well as in combat.
- Test Map equipment overrides live only in session config via `applyTestEquipment`; never write test selections to shop saves, and keep original combat cadence separate from visual gestures.
- Held equipment and its hand overlay must share one grip transform; tune asset grip coordinates, not combat hitboxes, to correct visual attachment.
- When establishing a weakest starter, derive the minimum adjustment from existing catalogue minima and keep bar normalization separate from combat values; preserve other champions and later evolutions.
- Shop capability bars are presentation-only heuristics in `shopStats.ts`, based on existing geometry and abilities; never use them as combat values. Evolution purchase-state squares use the pure `evolutionState` helper so affordability and sequential unlocks stay consistent.
- Weapon combat tuning (cadence, reach, knockback, ranged-only, ring bounces, dagger poison) lives in `weapons.ts`; shop attack-speed bars read the same `WEAPON_COOLDOWN_MUL` table. Test Map uses the `none` id for unequipped slots.
- Gameplay PNG weapons/effects load through `combatWeaponPng.ts`; grip-normalized drawings are shared by held and flying art. Keep shop assets untouched, capture projectile evolution on launch, and drive ranged gestures from successful launch timestamps rather than projectile presence.
- Shared thrust geometry and ground-contact timing live in `weaponAttack.ts`; ground hazards gate both rendering and damage on `impactAt`, and mace second hits must not alter crack size or rotation.
- Directional weapon art resolves its rotation after body mirroring; smooth visual aim separately from captured collision aim to prevent reversed tips. Weapon ground water belongs in the ground pass, and axe-specific full stuns use a separate timer from plague slows.
- Sword art uses the supplied PNG grip/height (`SWORD_ART`) and animated hand transform; never fixed offsets.
- Staff specials live in `fireStaffSpecial`/`updateStaff` (`equipment.ts`): every staff charges a timed special (cooldowns in `STAFF_SPECIAL_COOLDOWN`) and the rear-staff cast pose/light are driven only by `staffVisualAt` via `staffCastPose` (`equipmentFx.ts`); plague casts no longer animate the staff. Staff ids stay stable (`fire` = Wind Staff, `bush` = Stone Staff) to preserve purchases.
- The Staff of Moses blessing applies to exactly one attack: `castPlague` reads `pendingBlessEvo` into the swing before `weaponOnCast` spends it, and everything that attack spawns carries `blessEvo`/`blessId`/`empowered` (thrown weapons glow in flight). Every hit site calls `applyBlessing` before damage (I: -50% max HP, II: -75% of remaining, III: kill; once per foe per attack). `isToughFoe` (Ramses, or any enemy with `data.enemyChampion`) is exempt from blessing, bleeding and brawl max-HP damage, and survives Stone Staff weakened. The staff charge light follows the drawn orb via `setStaffCrownPoint` (set in `drawMosesNew`).
- Divine Light: one `stafflight` beam; every foe whose feet fall in `inLightCone` (matches the drawn art) gets `blindUntil`/`brawlUntil` and runs `brawlTick` (engine.ts) instead of its AI: walks to the nearest other brawler, stops at arm's length and strikes with its own `MELEE_ATTACKS` animation (lunge for kinds without one); damage lands mid-swing. Never snap enemies together. Supplied staff effect PNGs (`combat-staff-*`) are drawn in `drawStaffSpecial` at reduced alpha.
- Staff FX layering (GameCanvas): `isGroundWeaponFx` (crystal `staffring`, burning `firering`, whirlpool, mace impact) draws under everyone; `staffwave` draws after the depth-sorted pass, above NPCs; the Wind tornado sits 1 px below Moses so it sorts in front of him. Iron's `data.metal` tint and Diamond's `data.bleedDps` (ticked in `weaponDot`) last until death; blind eyes and blood drips are drawn by `drawEnemyStatusMarks` (eye points per kind are measured on the sprites in `ENEMY_EYE`; add one for a new enemy).
- Never tint enemies with `ctx.filter`: their sprites are many draw calls and a filter runs on each, which made Stone/Iron foes lag badly. Iron uses `beginTintLayer`/`endTintLayer` (full-size GPU buffer, only a small box around the foe cleared/tinted with source-atop). Never copy that buffer into another canvas or use "saturation"-style blends there: the per-frame GPU readback made it lag even worse. Dead foes leave a `corpse` visual hazard holding a snapshot of the entity; GameCanvas swaps the snapshot into the depth list with `data.dyingU` and fades it through the tint layer (never by setting `ctx.globalAlpha` around a sprite: sprite code sets its own alpha), under a `deathpuff` dust cloud that knows the foe's `kind`. Stone statues are painted once into their own small canvas (`beginStatue`/`endStatue`) and then only stamped by `drawStatue`. A petrified foe (`petrify` in `equipment.ts`) is skipped by the engine before `animT` advances and pinned to `stoneX`/`stoneY`, so its pose, route, attacks and position all freeze.
- Moses facing: weapon aim keeps its target for `AIM_HOLD_SECONDS` and only switches to a clearly closer foe; directional attacks lock facing for `FACE_LOCK_SECONDS` so walking doesn't flip him back and forth.
